02 — OUR STORY

THIS IS MUZ.

My interest in clothing started at a very young age. Whenever possible, I always wanted to wear branded clothes, and I was fascinated by the way different clothes, colours, cuts and designs could change the way a person looked and felt.

Over time, I started experimenting with my own clothes. I realised that instead of simply buying ready-made shirts, I enjoyed choosing the fabric myself and having shirts tailored exactly the way I wanted.

That gradually became my own style. I began experimenting with different cuts, curves, collars and small details that made a shirt feel different from an ordinary ready-made shirt.

People around me started noticing. They would ask about my shirts and sometimes ask me to have similar shirts tailored for them. I would select the fabric and colours according to their preferences and create something that suited them. At first, I only did this for people close to me.

Eventually, I thought: Why not do this professionally?

That thought became MUZ ORIGINALS.

MUZ is not simply a clothing brand I decided to create one day. It grew naturally from my love for clothing, experimenting with my own style and the encouragement I received from people around me.

Even the MUZ logo has a personal history. I had the idea for that symbol since I was a child. Whenever I imagined creating something of my own, I wanted it to carry that identity.

Today, MUZ ORIGINALS is my attempt to turn that long-standing passion into something real — starting with shirts, built around personal style, careful fabric selection, distinctive cuts and details, and the belief that clothing should feel like it belongs to the person wearing it.
